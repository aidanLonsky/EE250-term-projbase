# W1T4 - Fit on Model

**Fit on model.** Departurn delay $D$ can be negative, so a lognormal cannot decribe all of it. Record $p_0 = P(D>0)$ then fit a lognormal distribution to the flights with $D>0$ only. Keep $p_0$ and the fitted parameters, because you will reuse them in Week 3.
