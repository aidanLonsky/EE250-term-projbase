# Topic

Topics are areas of interest or conceptual lenses.

Use this type to gather knowledge without implying ownership, completion, or performance expectations.

*I've found Topics are nice when used as "ledgers" for collecting ideas about a Concept or Theme; They imply a certain "organic growth" process, sort of a "Git log" of the thinking process*
